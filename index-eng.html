01 / About us

A meeting point for art, people and ideas.

At the heart of Beco do Batman, CasaBeco connects the independent arts scene with participatory experiences, creative initiatives and new opportunities.

More than an exhibition space, CasaBeco is a place for connection, experimentation and collaboration. It brings together works by different artists, painting experiences, workshops, independent fashion, original products and activities that invite visitors to take part.

CasaBeco brings together two complementary worlds: Art, with creative experiences, artworks and supplies; and Café, with drinks, food and a welcoming place to meet and stay awhile.

Our location places us in a neighbourhood known for street art and cultural tourism, creating opportunities for projects and partners to build visibility and connections.

The Alley

At the heart of São Paulo’s urban art scene.

CasaBeco is located in Beco do Batman, one of the city’s main meeting points for art, culture and creativity.

Located in Vila Madalena, Beco do Batman is an open-air urban art gallery, known for its graffiti murals, artistic interventions and ever-changing walls. Since the 1980s, it has brought together artists, visitors and people interested in different expressions of urban culture, becoming a landmark in São Paulo’s cultural and tourism landscape.

This is where CasaBeco is based. Its main entrance opens directly onto Beco do Batman, welcoming visitors into one of Brazil’s best-known urban art circuits. The location connects the CasaBeco experience with the artists, tourists, residents and creators who move through the area.

Alongside its main entrance from the Beco, CasaBeco has a secondary entrance at Rua Aspicuelta, 99, for production, staff and operational support. This setup helps organise events, workshops, installations and special projects while keeping the venue directly connected to the public and the cultural life of the surrounding area.

More than an address, CasaBeco is part of an area where art unfolds in the streets and finds new ways to reach people.

Commercial opportunities

Brands and makers

Ways to showcase and sell products or host a workshop at CasaBeco.

  • Workshop table rental+

    Reserve a table for two hours for R$ 300. It can accommodate up to 10 participants, depending on the activity. The artist or instructor sets the ticket price, manages registration and payments, and keeps 100% of the proceeds. They also bring the materials and lead the workshop. CasaBeco books the table and remains open to the public during the workshop; the time is arranged when booking.

  • Display niches+

    Each niche measures 45 × 45 × 30 cm and costs R$ 300 per month, with the option to renew. Schedule your delivery and set up the display with the CasaBeco team.

  • Clothing rails for fashion brands+

    Display up to three garments for R$ 300 per month. Schedule the delivery and arrange the display with the CasaBeco team.

  • Temporary pop-ups and launches+

    Book CasaBeco for a launch, collection presentation or brand activation, either open to the public or exclusively for invited guests. The time and duration are arranged in advance; pricing is quoted according to the format and event requirements.

CasaBeco handles sales of products displayed in niches and on clothing rails and deducts 22% from the sales value: 17% for taxes and 5% for card-processing fees. The remaining amount is paid on the last business day of the month following the sale.

Events and experiences

Tailored experiences

Art experiences to enjoy individually or as part of a private group event.

  • Canvas painting · Art Experience+

    Choose a self-guided session for R$ 120 per person or an artist-guided session for R$ 250 per person. The experience includes a canvas, easel, paint palette, brushes, paints and an apron.

  • Graffiti · ZIV Walls+

    The guided spray-painting experience costs R$ 150 for 15 minutes and includes spray paint, an apron and gloves. The graffiti course costs R$ 250 and covers painting techniques, spray nozzle types and the marks they produce, with an artist as your guide.

  • Eco-bag customisation+

    Customise your eco-bag for R$ 120 per person in a self-guided session or R$ 250 with an artist. The eco-bag and painting materials are included.

  • Private events for groups of up to 20+

    Book CasaBeco for a celebration, birthday or team gathering. The group chooses an experience and has the venue exclusively for its guests. Café purchases, a food station or an open bar can be added; the quote depends on the activity, number of participants, and selected food and drink options.

Canvas painting and eco-bag customisation last two hours and are available from 10 am to 7 pm or by appointment. Café menu orders are charged separately.
